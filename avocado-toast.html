Avocado Toast with Cherry Tomatoes



Description:

Flavor Profile: Fresh, creamy, and savory. Rich mashed avocado is balanced by the sweet, juicy pop of blistered cherry tomatoes and a hint of tanginess from lemon juice. Culinary Context: A modern brunch staple rooted in café culture, celebrating simple, wholesome ingredients combined for a quick and nutritious meal.
1
  • Ingredients
    • Produce: 1 ripe avocado, 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, fresh cilantro or parsley
    • Bakery: 2 slices of thick crusty sourdough bread
    • Pantry: Extra virgin olive oil, lemon juice, red pepper flakes
    • Seasonings: Sea salt, freshly cracked black pepper
  • Instructions
    1. Toast bread: Toast the sourdough slices until golden brown and crispy.
    2. Prep avocado: Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Mash with a fork, then mix in a splash of lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
    3. Prepare tomatoes: Halve the cherry tomatoes and toss lightly with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
    4. Assemble: Spread the mashed avocado evenly over the toast slices, top with the seasoned cherry tomatoes, and finish with a sprinkle of red pepper flakes and herbs.
