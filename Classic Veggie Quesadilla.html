Classic Garlic Butter Pasta

This is a simple and delicious garlic butter pasta recipe.


Description: Flavor Profile Savory, cheesy, and smoky. Melty cheese binds together sautéed peppers, onions, and black beans, enhanced by a warm hint of earthy cumin and mild chili.
Culinary Context Inspired by traditional Mexican street food culture, the quesadilla is an adaptable comfort food staple designed to transform everyday ingredients into a fast, satisfying meal.

  • Ingredients
    • Pasta: 200g spaghetti or linguine
    • Sauce: 2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp olive oil, 4 cloves garlic (sliced)
    • Seasonings: ½ tsp red pepper flakes, salt, black pepper
    • Toppings: Fresh parsley or basil, grated Parmesan cheese
  • Instructions
    1. Boil the pasta: Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
    2. Sauté garlic: Melt butter with olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook sliced garlic for 1–2 minutes until golden.
    3. Combine: Add cooked pasta, red pepper flakes, and a splash of reserved pasta water. Toss well until glossy.
    4. Serve: Season with salt and pepper, then top with fresh herbs and Parmesan before serving hot!
